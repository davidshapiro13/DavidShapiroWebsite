David Shapiro is a multidisciplinary creative and computer scientist based in Somerville, MA. He works across filmmaking, acting, software development, and illustration, combining storytelling with practical technical execution.

He graduated from Brandeis University in 2024 with a double major in Computer Science and Film, Television & Interactive Media, and is currently completing his M.S. in Computer Science at Tufts University.

College

Brandeis University (2020 - 2024)

At Brandeis, David majored in Computer Science and Film, Television & Interactive Media while minoring in Mathematics and Creative Writing.

While there, David worked at the Sound and Image Media Studios, drew comics for the Brandeis Hoot Newspaper and volunteered through Waltham Group working as a math teacher, doing farm work and volunteering with adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities.
